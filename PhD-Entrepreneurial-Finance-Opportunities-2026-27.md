Your title — *Financial Strategy Fit, Investor Heterogeneity, and Startup Scaling* — is **strong but currently too broad to be fundable as written**. Three pillars, multi-method, and "dynamic" is roughly three PhDs. Every supervisor will ask you to cut.

**Universal edits (do these regardless of supervisor):**
